## Substack

Title: Introducing Dhara Mitra: your farm, in one place

Subtitle: An offline Android app for everyday farm records, in Kannada and English.

Cover: substack-cover.png

Farm records are made up of small details: what work happened, who worked, what was paid, how much was harvested, and what a buyer still owes.

Over time, those details become hard to keep together. Finding a payment or checking when an activity last happened should be straightforward.

That is the idea behind **Dhara Mitra**, an Android app I built to help manage everyday farm records.

### What you can do

Record farm activities and notes. Track worker attendance, wages, advances and payments. Keep expenses, harvests, sales and buyer payments together, with workflows for arecanut and coconut.

The calendar lets you return to a date and see what happened. You can add or correct records, and search recorded activities by keyword.

The goal is simple: spend less time looking for details and make it easier to understand what's happening on the farm.

### Built for everyday use

Dhara Mitra works offline, supports Kannada and English, and does not require a login for everyday use. Records stay on your phone.

You can export a local backup and transfer farm records to another phone. Home provides summaries and quick actions; Calendar takes you back to the details.

### From idea to app

Coding agents helped me turn the idea into working software. What interested me was the chance to build something useful around an everyday task: keeping farm records organised.

Dhara Mitra is now available on Android. Download **DharaMitra.apk** from the GitHub release below.
