# Dhara Mitra 0.3.5

- Labour reports now offer Day, Week, Month, Year and Custom periods with a clear selected-date explanation, exact boundaries and previous/next controls. Weeks run Monday to Sunday and years run January to December. The complete final day is included. PDF and Excel exports follow the same period and worker selection; period-end dues include earlier unpaid wages.
- Calendar sale records show “Sold to {name}” when the buyer name is filled in.
- Home Money shows Income, Total expenses and Due for labours in that order. Income and expenses include records through today. Earned labour wages count once as expenses; paying those wages does not add another expense.
- Things bought for farm is available from Home Farm Tools and Calendar additions. Record the item, purchase date, cost and notes. Purchases can be edited or deleted and update farm expenses and reports automatically.
- English and Kannada support for the new purchase flow and buyer titles. Existing records stay in the same farm database; no database migration is required.
- Updated phone-number parsing metadata to 9.0.29 after reviewing the Dependabot patch and passing checks. The unrelated Supabase upgrade was closed for this release because it requires account initialization changes.

Validation: automated date-boundary, purchase-accounting and widget coverage; narrow-screen checks in English and Kannada. Android CI also analyzes, tests, audits dependencies and builds the release APK.
