# Dhara Mitra 0.3.2

- Use a + chip to add a named work type or crop. Categories are chosen as chips, including required crop selection for harvests and sales.
- Sales support multiple crops for one buyer, with each crop’s quantity/unit, one exact total income, and optional Additional Notes at the end. Existing single-crop sales stay editable.
- Excel includes a SaleItems sheet linked to the Income sheet by sale_record_id; total income is recorded once per sale, not once per crop.
- Long-press built-in or custom farm-activity suggestions to remove them after confirmation. Saved records remain unchanged. Add a removed custom suggestion again using +.
- New users receive a tutorial after choosing their language and saving their first farm. More → Tutorial replays the training in English or Kannada, with an option to try each real screen.
- Home, Calendar and More navigation is available from feature pages. Untouched forms navigate immediately; edited forms offer Keep editing or Discard and leave. Successful saves and automatically saved attendance do not trigger warnings.
- Existing farms, workers, wages, crop sales and history remain stored with schema 6 and the existing signing identity.

Install over the current app. Do not uninstall or clear app storage.
