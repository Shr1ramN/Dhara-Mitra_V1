# Dhara Mitra V3 (0.3.0)

- A simpler Home in the familiar orange/green style, focused on attendance, worker dues, farm activity and harvests.
- Tap a calendar date to open three choices immediately: Labour Management, Farm Activity and Harvest.
- All attendance, wage settlements, dues, worker loans and weekly/monthly/yearly Labour Reports stay together in Labour Management.
- Full-day, half-day and absent attendance save automatically. Daily-wage and other workers retain exact monetary balances.
- Farm Activity combines irrigation, fertiliser, spray and custom labelled descriptions. Harvest records your crop and quantity in bags or kilograms.
- Farm setup/edit asks what you grow, with editable custom crops and no required plots/tree counts.
- More includes Light, Dark and System default appearance, English/Kannada, farm switching, backup/restore, app lock, database export and new Excel export.
- Excel includes readable labour, settlements, loans, farm activity and harvest sheets, plus all raw historical tables. It is an editable snapshot; workbook edits are not imported into the app.
- Market prices, weather/rain reminders, sales/buyers, farm loans, land parcels, insurance and schemes are removed from navigation. Their existing records remain stored and included in backups/raw exports. Worker loans remain available.

## Updating safely

Install DharaMitra.apk over the existing app. Do not uninstall or clear app storage. V3 retains the package name, signing certificate and database schema; existing labour, attendance, money, farm and historical records are preserved. A backup outside the phone remains useful.

These APKs retain the project's existing development signing key.
