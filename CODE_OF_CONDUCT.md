# Code of Conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome people across languages, backgrounds and levels of technical experience. Give feedback about the work, listen to other perspectives and ask before sharing personal information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention and publishing someone's private information without permission are unacceptable in project spaces.

For a conduct concern, ask [the maintainer](https://github.com/Shr1ramN) for a private reporting channel. Share the relevant links and details privately. The maintainer may remove harmful content, issue warnings or restrict participation based on the circumstances.
